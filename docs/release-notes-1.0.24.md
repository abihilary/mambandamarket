# 1.0.24 (build 28)

Play Console → "What's new". Each block is under Google's 500-character

## en-US

A new look, and a real search.

• Search is its own screen now — filter by category, price and condition, and sort how you like.
• The search bar stays with you as you scroll the feed.
• Favourites are grouped by category and open straight to the item.
• See how far away an item is, and sort by distance.
• Sign in with Google without leaving the app.
• Redesigned home, account and welcome screens.
