A cleaner account screen, and better use of bigger screens.

• New Seller progress card: listings published against your plan, at a glance.
• Totals you can see: earnings, active listings and messages.
• Tablets and large phones now fit more items per row instead of stretching them.
• Search reads properly in light mode.
