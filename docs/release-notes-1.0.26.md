# 1.0.26 (build 30)

Play Console → "What's new". Each block is under Google's 500-character
limit. Paste per language.

## en-US

A new promotional section on the home screen, under the banner: an offer
can now carry a headline, a description and real buttons that take you
straight to a product, a category, or the details of the offer without
leaving the app.

Below it, a Trending row showing what people are looking at right now — and
it stays out of the way when there is nothing trending.

Also: updates now go through Google Play, and you can reach support from
your account.

## de-DE

Ein neuer Aktionsbereich auf der Startseite, direkt unter dem Banner: Ein
