# 1.0.27 (build 31)

Play Console → "What's new". Each block is under Google's 500-character
limit. Paste per language.

Written to cover everything since 1.0.25, which is what `update.latest_build`
still points at — so it reads correctly whether or not 1.0.26 was rolled out.

## en-US

Ask us to ship something for you. Pick a product from Mambanda or paste a
link to one we don't carry, tell us where it's coming from and going to, and
follow it from your phone as it moves.

Support is now a conversation. Ask a question and it lands in Messages, where
you can see the reply and come back to it later.

Also: a new promotional section and a Trending row on the home screen, and
updates now go through Google Play.

## de-DE

wohin, und verfolgen Sie es unterwegs auf Ihrem Telefon.
