# 1.0.47 (build 53)

Play Console → "What's new". Each block is under Google's 500-character
limit. Paste per language.

## en-US

• Faster on slow or no data: the home feed, item pages, favourites, your account and your chats open at once from what your phone already has, then update quietly.
• Chats load only new messages instead of the whole thread, and stop checking while the app is closed, saving data and battery.
• Photos in chats are kept on your phone, so they don't download again.
• Offline, the app tells you what you're seeing is a saved copy, and never lets you pay on old information.
