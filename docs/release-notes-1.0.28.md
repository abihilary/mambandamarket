# 1.0.28 (build 32)

Play Console → "What's new". Each block is under Google's 500-character
limit. Paste per language.

One change since 1.0.27: the Trending row. Everything else in 1.0.27 —
shipping requests, support conversations, the promo section — is unchanged.

## en-US

Trending got a proper look. Every product now shows where it ranks, how many
people saved it, how many have looked at it, when it went up and who is
selling it.

Three ways to look: what's trending now, what's trending near you, and what's
trending in the kind of thing you save most.

## fr-FR
