# Category-Specific Product Presentation Prototypes (All Categories)

We have successfully created comprehensive interactive UI prototypes for **Mambanda Market** covering **all major categories**, inspired by Kleinanzeigen / mobile.de design patterns.

## Expanded Category Presentations Built

1. **Home Feed (`_PrototypeHomeFeed`)**:
   - Location search header (`Suche in Aalen`), category filter pills, badged product cards with "Direkt kaufen", discounts (`-14%`), and bottom navigation bar.

2. **Electronics & Phones (`_PrototypeElectronicsDetail`)**:
   - Specifications: *Marke, Modell, Farbe, Speicher (GB), Zustand, Betriebssystem, Arbeitsspeicher (RAM)*.
   - Buyer Protection banner with payment logos (G Pay, VISA, Mastercard, Klarna, PayPal).

3. **Vehicles & Cars (`_PrototypeVehicleDetail`)**:
   - Specifications: *Marke, Modell, Kilometerstand, Kraftstoffart, Getriebe, Leistung, Erstzulassung, HU bis*.
   - Equipment checklists (*Außenausstattung*, *Innenausstattung*, *Sicherheit*), financing link, and seller follow card.

4. **Real Estate / Immobilien (`_PrototypeRealEstateDetail`)**:
   - Specifications: *Immobilientyp, Zimmer, Wohnfläche, Etage, Warmmiete, Nebenkosten, Kaution, Verfügbar ab, Energieausweis*.
   - Features checklist (*Balkon, Einbauküche, Aufzug, Tiefgarage, Fußbodenheizung*).

5. **Home & Garden / Haus & Garten (`_PrototypeHomeGardenDetail`)**:
   - Specifications: *Kategorie, Art, Material, Zustand, Maße (LxBxH)*.
   - Pickup and local delivery details.

6. **Fashion & Beauty / Mode & Beauty (`_PrototypeFashionDetail`)**:
   - Specifications: *Marke, Modell, Größe, Farbe, Zustand*.
   - Shipping options and condition details.

---

## How to Test and Validate in the App

1. Open the app and navigate to the **Account / Meins** tab.
2. Tap on **"Category Presentations Prototype"**.
3. Use the horizontal tab bar at the top to test all 6 category prototypes seamlessly.
